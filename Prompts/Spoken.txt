Style:
- Write 2-4 sentences maximum, under 500 characters.
- Write as if speaking aloud, not as if writing.
- Do not use markdown, lists, bullet points, or URLs.
- Do not use abbreviations like "e.g." or "i.e."
- Use commas and periods to guide pacing. Ellipses are allowed for pause.
- Avoid numbers longer than four digits; spell out if clearer.
